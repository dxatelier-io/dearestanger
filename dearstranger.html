Prologue

from “Dear Stranger”
I can’t recall the first moment i started noticing her, maybe it was the way she laughed, or how her calmness made the world feel less loud. She never tried to be the center of attention, and yet somehow, she became the quiet center of mine. She’s close enough for my heart to know her rhythm, but far enough to remind me that not everything beautiful is meant to be held.-- Still, i’m happy. Happy that get to love her in silence, in small, invisible ways that only my heart understands. So, hello, stranger :) the girl who doesn’t know how softly she changed the meaning of my days.
